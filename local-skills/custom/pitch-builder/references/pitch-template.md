# Pitch Template (5–7 slides, 2–4 minutes)

Design rules: dark or white background consistently, one font family, 40pt+ headlines, max 15 words per slide, one visual per slide, product screenshots over stock photos.

## Slide 1 — Hook
- On slide: one sentence or one number, large. Optional single image.
- Say (10–15 s): the hook line. No team intro.

## Slide 2 — Problem
- On slide: user persona name + one-line pain + one stat with source.
- Say (15–20 s): who suffers, how often, what they do today, why that fails.

## Slide 3 — Demo
- On slide: product name + tagline only.
- Do (60–90 s): switch to live app; run the demo script; land the wow moment; pause.

## Slide 4 — How it works
- On slide: architecture diagram, max 6 boxes, arrows left-to-right, sponsor tech logos/labels on their boxes. Highlight the one hard thing you built.
- Say (20–30 s): "When you <action>, we <step 1>, <step 2>, and <step 3>. The hard part was <x>; we solved it with <y>."

## Slide 5 — Impact
- On slide: one metric (tested at venue if possible) + one user quote.
- Say (15–20 s): who benefits, how much, how you know.

## Slide 6 — What's next
- On slide: 3 bullets max: next feature, next users, next milestone.
- Say (10–15 s): include anything mocked, framed as next step.

## Slide 7 — Team + ask
- On slide: names, roles, live URL as QR code, one ask.
- Say (10 s): the ask. Then "Happy to take questions."

## Script skeleton
```
[S1] <hook line>. /
[S2] <user> <pain>. Today they <workaround>, which <why it fails>. <stat>. /
[S3 → switch to app] This is <product>. <user> <starts flow>. … [wow] … / (pause) /
<proof line>. 
[S4] Under the hood: <3 steps>. The hard part was <x>. <sponsor tech> powers <y>.
[S5] <metric>. <quote>.
[S6] Next: <1>, <2>, <3>.
[S7] We're <names>. <ask>. Thank you.
```

## Time budgets
| Slot | Hook | Problem | Demo | How | Impact | Next | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 min | 10 | 15 | 55 | 15 | 10 | 8 | 7 |
| 3 min | 12 | 20 | 80 | 25 | 18 | 13 | 12 |
| 4 min | 15 | 25 | 100 | 35 | 25 | 20 | 20 |
Shift seconds toward whichever beat maps to the heaviest rubric criterion.
