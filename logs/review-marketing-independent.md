## Scope and method

Read the rendered worked-example sections for one skill in each of the 17 domains: MAR-005, MBR-002, MCA-002, MEC-004, MEM-009, MME-005, MOP-006, MPH-002, MPU-010, MRE-001, MSA-010, MSC-004, MSE-005, MSL-001, MSS-007, MSV-002, MVA-009. Compared the supplied fictional evidence with decisions and concrete artifacts; inspected methods for distinct decisions rather than renamed generic checklists. The reviewed shared instructions require current external requirements to be checked in authoritative documentation, preserve unknowns, and distinguish draft proposals from observed outcomes. No external current platform facts were asserted or verified during this review. The examples inspected do not contain quantitative calculations requiring arithmetic correction; numbering and qualitative cohort comparisons are not numeric performance claims.

## Corrections in this review
- MSE-005: discontinuation alone does not establish that an item is unavailable. The fictional input now explicitly supplies both discontinued status and unavailability, matching the example's action and acceptance criterion.
- MAR-002: a useful parts catalog was not established by the input. The artifact now treats the catalog as a candidate and requires confirmation of its existence and contents before linking.
- MRE-001: the example probe presupposed a workaround and difficulty. It now asks whether the task was difficult or worked as expected, and asks about consequences only if difficulty is reported.
- MPU-003: a service-side failure did not establish that the entire service was unavailable. The draft now describes a service-side problem and leaves the supported recovery action to verification.
