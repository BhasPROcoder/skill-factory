## ARC-001 ordinary

Request: Fictional repository Moss expects two folders F-A and F-B in delivery Wave. Receiving record R reports one opened box containing both labeled folders and an unlisted loose photograph X. No authority to accept additions is supplied.

```text
Transfer: Moss delivery Wave; expected list F-A/F-B and receiving record R.
Container register:
Container | Labels | Observed state | Content evidence
Received box | Box label unspecified | Opened according to R | F-A, F-B and loose photograph X
Reconciliation:
Expected reference and unit | Received reference | Finding | Evidence
F-A folder | R/F-A | Matched by supplied label | Receiving record R
F-B folder | R/F-B | Matched by supplied label | Receiving record R
No expected entry | R/X photograph | Unexpected item; identity and acceptance unresolved | R lists loose X
Identifier links: Preserve F-A, F-B and X; no new catalog identities assigned.
Open checks: Receiving owner confirms whether X belongs to Wave and who may accept or redirect it; obtain source correspondence rather than assign it to a folder by proximity.
Disposition: Both listed folder labels observed under supplied evidence; their internal completeness was not assessed. X remains separate. No ownership or accession acceptance determined.
```

Result: Expected units reconciled without equating one box to two folders; unlisted item preserved separately. No blocker.
