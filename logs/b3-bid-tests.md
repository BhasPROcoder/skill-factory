# Procurement series: independent text executions

Reviewer: warehouse-series author (not a BID author). The inputs below were composed as new fictional requests and run against the corresponding BID instructions. Outputs are the reviewer's responses. They do not constitute supplier verification, an actual offer comparison on live data, a release, document edits or a cross-model trial.

## BID-001 — Ordinarily comparable delivered offers

Request: Fictional depot Lime needs 21 identical tags delivered to its depot this week. Both vendors confirm the requested specification and delivery. Mint quotes whole packs of 8 at 16 tokens per pack, freight included. Fir offers individual tags at 2 tokens and charges 9 tokens freight per order. No other charges or surplus credit. Compare.

Basis: 21 specified tags delivered this week to Lime, all costs in fictional tokens.
Offer bridge:
Offer | Demand / order / surplus | Quoted component | Adjustments and source | Evaluated result
Mint | 21 / 24 / 3 | 3 whole packs × 16 = 48 | Freight included; no surplus credit | 48 tokens
Fir | 21 / 21 / 0 | 21 × 2 = 42 | 9 freight per order from Fir | 51 tokens
Comparability: Both meet supplied specification and timing. Totals reflect the order that can actually be bought.
Clarifications: None needed for this bounded cost comparison.
Disposition: Mint costs 3 tokens less on this basis and leaves 3 surplus tags. This does not award an order.

## BID-001 — Missing pack definition

Request: Fictional depot Gull needs 10 sheets delivered. A offers “one carton, 20 tokens” without carton contents, delivery terms or applicable minimum. B offers any quantity, 3 tokens per sheet with delivery. Both meet the sheet specification. Can A be called cheaper?

Basis: 10 sheets delivered; tokens; delivery window unspecified.
Offer bridge:
Offer | Demand / order / surplus | Quoted component | Adjustments and source | Evaluated result
A | 10 / unknown / unknown | 20 per carton × purchasable cartons | Delivery inclusion unknown | Indeterminate
B | 10 / 10 / 0 | 10 × 3 = 30 | Delivery included | 30 tokens
Comparability: A's pack basis, minimum and delivery cost prevent a comparable total. B has a bounded delivered cost; neither timing result is established.
Clarifications: How many sheets are in A's carton and what is the minimum purchase? Is delivery included or separately priced? Is a specific delivery window required?
Disposition: Withhold ranking; a single-carton price is not proof that 10 sheets can be obtained for 20 tokens.

## BID-001 — Explicit mandatory scope gate

Request: Fictional workshop Grove needs four boxes by its opening event; the date is mandatory. Vendor P offers four at 7 tokens each with delivery after the event. Q offers four at 9 tokens each delivered before it. Both meet every other stated requirement; no extras.

Basis: Four boxes delivered before Grove's opening event; same fictional tokens.
Offer bridge:
Offer | Demand / order / surplus | Quoted component | Adjustments and source | Evaluated result
P | 4 / 4 / 0 | 4 × 7 = 28 | Delivery included | 28 tokens, fails timing
Q | 4 / 4 / 0 | 4 × 9 = 36 | Delivery included | 36 tokens, meets timing
Comparability: P is not eligible on the supplied delivery gate; Q satisfies it. The price difference is not a saving available on compliant scope.
Clarifications: None unless P submits a revised lead time backed by evidence.
Disposition: Only Q meets the supplied scope and timing; preserve P's original quote without awarding either order.

## BID-002 — Exact evidence for bounded recommendation

Request: Fictional exhibit house Aster wants its review lead to decide on replacing sign holder J/Reed with K/Moss for booth W only. The only stated requirements are a matte surface and a side-loading slot. Review sheet L signed by the exhibit inspector, explicitly for K/Moss, records a matte surface and a side-loading slot. The lead has release authority but has not acted.

Change boundary: Replace J/Reed with K/Moss only for booth W; no other use is in scope.
Requirements:
Requirement | Baseline | Candidate evidence | Finding | Open action
Surface | Matte required | L records matte on K/Moss | Supported | Retain L
Loading | Side slot required | L records side-loading slot on K/Moss | Supported | Retain L
Downstream handling: Keep K/Moss identified separately for W pending recorded release; attach the comparison and L to the affected order.
Proposed disposition: Recommend acceptance for W on the supplied requirements; no blanket approval for other uses or revisions.
Release evidence: The review lead is authorized; no recorded decision supplied.
Next gate: Lead reviews and records disposition for K/Moss at W before release.

## BID-002 — Supplier claim with no identifiable baseline

Request: Fictional seller Rose says its new part is “equivalent” and asks to ship immediately. No order, revision, part number, use or records are supplied.

Change boundary: Baseline and candidate unidentified; affected lot, order and use unknown.
Requirements:
Requirement | Baseline | Candidate evidence | Finding | Open action
Equivalence | No requirements supplied | Supplier's general assertion | Untestable | Obtain ordered and proposed identities, requirements and matching evidence
Downstream handling: Keep any proposed alternate separate from released stock until identity and scope are established; no shipment instruction issued.
Proposed disposition: Hold; equivalence cannot be assessed without a baseline and identified alternate.
Release evidence: None supplied.
Next gate: What exact order item, revision and use are affected? Which alternate and evidence apply? Who owns the disposition and release?

## BID-002 — Evidence for a different revision

Request: Fictional supplier Drift offers F/revision New for order O, which requires fabric with a woven hanging loop. The earlier F/Old sample record shows a loop. Drift says New is unchanged, but provides no change record or new inspection. O's buyer may request evidence but cannot waive the loop requirement.

Change boundary: Ordered fabric with woven loop, order O; ordered revision unknown; alternate F/New, only O in scope.
Requirements:
Requirement | Baseline | Candidate evidence | Finding | Open action
Hanging loop | Woven loop required | Documented on F/Old; supplier claims New unchanged | F/New unverified | Obtain a New-specific inspection or documented applicability of Old evidence to New
Downstream handling: Record the distinction between Old and New; keep alternate pending for O. Do not relabel Old evidence as New.
Proposed disposition: Hold F/New; the evidence gap does not yet establish either compliance or a confirmed defect.
Release evidence: No recorded release; buyer has evidence-request authority only.
Next gate: Obtain candidate identity-linked evidence and baseline details; route any confirmed mismatch to an authorized exception reviewer.

## BID-003 — Withdrawal affects one lot

Issuer addendum Birch, confirmed for K/North, explicitly withdraws only North's printed-sample requirement; it says its drawing requirement is unchanged. Identify updates. No files have been edited.

Packet: K; North and corresponding South entries in baseline; applicable notice Birch covers North.
Change ledger:
Locator | Before | After | Evidence and disposition
North/printed sample | Required | Withdrawn | Birch explicitly withdraws North sample; apply to North only
Response impact:
Artifact | Required revision or check | Owner or proposed role | Status
Draft North checklist | Remove printed sample from required items; retain drawing | Proposed response owner | Pending edit
Draft South checklist | Check that sample and drawing entries remain intact | Proposed response owner | Pending check; no revision called for by Birch
Unchanged: North's electronic drawing; both supplied South requirements.
Open questions: None for this bounded change.
Release note: Change handoff ready; no checklist edited, validated, sent or submitted.

## BID-003 — Unofficial change summary

Packet: Kite response to Lake; Baseline A; no authenticated addendum supplied.
Change ledger:

Response impact:
Artifact | Required revision or check | Owner or proposed role | Status

## Findings and limits

The bid-cost procedure preserved compulsory surplus, unknown freight and hard scope gates. The substitution procedure separated revision-specific evidence from authority to release. The addendum procedure traced authoritative changes to response artifacts without treating download order or hearsay as precedence. No blocking defect arose in the nine text executions. These are bounded demonstrations by one independent agent, not evidence of performance on every procurement process or every model.
