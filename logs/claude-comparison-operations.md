## Generalization and claim review

Removed personal business names, age/location assumptions, private portfolio context, mandatory outreach instructions, provider rankings, country stereotypes, and claimed universal behavior. Removed numerical valuation multiples, fees, tax rates and thresholds, statutory deadlines, prescribed ownership pools, legal clause guarantees, grant amounts, funding probabilities, conversion claims, and medical-sounding workload cutoffs. None were treated as verified current facts. The generalized skills instead collect the actual context and direct changing requirements to the responsible official authority, governing documents, provider documentation, or qualified adviser.

All new examples explicitly identify fictional people or organizations. They use qualitative evidence states rather than calculated monetary results, so there are no numerical worked-example totals requiring arithmetic repair. Proposed owner roles in the diligence example are labeled as proposals. The fundraising example now explicitly supplies a demonstration, and the grant example explicitly supplies a prototype-development call purpose, so their outputs do not invent those premises.

High-risk entries collect jurisdiction without a silent default, state the knowledge-cutoff limitation, require current-source verification for dependent interpretations, and refer individualized legal, tax or financial conclusions to an appropriately qualified professional. These are preparation workflows; no live legal, tax, investment or medical guidance was produced. No source claims were independently researched as current law because no such current facts were retained.
