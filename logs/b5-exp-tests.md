## Accuracy, scope and uniqueness review

No blocking defect found in the eighteen cases. Numbers in these requests are record identities or test organization, not empirical claims, rates or computed quantities; no arithmetic is needed. Skill worked examples use explicit fictional records and no unsupported calculations. Production/access criteria are verify inputs; no current legal, software or conservation rule is prescribed.

Compared routing summaries with sf.py show: VEV-001 concerns evidence labeling within reconstruction scenes; EXP-001 concerns propagation of a changed claim across published derivatives. GLA-001/003 concern translation briefing and modality; EXP-002 adds exhibition reading paths, physical space and distinct rendered-fit approval. MSL-010 concerns arrival/access information; EXP-005 concerns interpretive prerequisites along routes. ARC-001 concerns collection intake scope; EXP-006 concerns loan-specific components, condition comparison and distinct return custody/closure evidence. SHP-001 reconciles shipment quantity/package assignments, without the loan-return interpretation. EXP-003 physical identity placement, EXP-004 visible session boundaries and EXP-005 route meaning have distinct triggers, evidence and output decisions; shared evidence discipline does not make their procedures interchangeable.

Limits: This independent text exercise is not a cross-model evaluation, installed exhibit audit, competent translation review, runtime kiosk test, physical handling assessment or professional compliance certification. Existing skill validation is structural and does not convert these findings into an audited registry status.
